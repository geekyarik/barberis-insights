# The owner's context is a second source of truth that can change analysis

Our clients' behaviour depends on things outside our control (migration and mobilisation, holidays and the run-up to them, air-raid alerts, competition), so Facts alone mislead. Context is stored as structured factors with a day or week period (optionally yearly), a scope, an expected direction and a treatment (annotate, exclude, adjust, control; for one client, suppress overdue). A calculation runs under a lens, a rule resolved to a frozen list of factors, and every run, measurement and hypothesis records it. Factors stay beliefs until a hypothesis checks them against the Facts, and a recurring factor's effect is measured from the shop's history by an analysis, never typed in. The `factors` model replaces today's free-text notes in Phase 5.
