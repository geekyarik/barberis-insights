# The owner's context is a second source of truth that can change analysis

Our clients' behaviour depends heavily on things outside our control (power outages, air-raid alerts, migration, the economy, holidays), so Facts alone mislead. Context is stored as structured factors with a period, scope, expected effects and a treatment (annotate, exclude, adjust, control), and every measurement and hypothesis records the lens of treatments it used. Factors stay beliefs until a hypothesis checks them against the Facts. To be confirmed in the `grill-with-docs` session before the `factors` model replaces today's free-text notes.
